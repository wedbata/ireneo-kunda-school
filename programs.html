Our Programs

Comprehensive education from early childhood through secondary school

Nursery School Program

Ages 3-5 years

Building Strong Foundations

Our nursery program provides a warm, nurturing environment where young children begin their educational journey. We focus on play-based learning that develops social, emotional, cognitive, and physical skills.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Early literacy and language development
  • Basic numeracy and problem-solving skills
  • Social interaction and emotional development
  • Fine and gross motor skill development
  • Creative expression through art, music, and play
  • Building confidence and independence

Daily Activities Include:

Learning Through Play

Songs & Rhymes

Art & Crafts

Story Time

Physical Activities

Group Activities

Primary School Program

Primary 1-8 (Ages 6-13 years)

Building Knowledge and Character

Our primary school program provides comprehensive education that builds strong academic foundations while nurturing students' curiosity, creativity, and character. We follow the national curriculum while incorporating modern teaching methods.

Core Subjects:

English Language

Reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills

Mathematics

Numeracy, problem-solving, and logical thinking

Science

General science, biology, and environmental studies

Social Studies

Geography, history, and civic education

Religious Education

Moral and spiritual development

Arts & PE

Creative arts, music, and physical education

Learning Approach:

  • Student-centered, interactive teaching methods
  • Continuous assessment and feedback
  • Homework and independent study support
  • Group projects and collaborative learning
  • Practical, hands-on activities
  • Regular parent-teacher communication

Secondary School Program

Senior 1-4 (Ages 14-17 years)

Preparing for the Future

Our secondary school program prepares students for national examinations and future academic or career pursuits. We offer rigorous academic instruction combined with career guidance and life skills development.

Academic Streams:

Science Stream

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics

Commercial Stream

Commerce, Accounting, Economics, Business Studies

Arts Stream

Literature, History, Geography, Religious Studies

Core Subjects (All Streams):

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Arabic Language
  • Physical Education
  • Computer Studies

Examination Preparation:

  • National Secondary School Certificate preparation
  • Regular mock examinations and assessments
  • Intensive revision and study support
  • Career counseling and guidance
  • University and college application support
