Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to enroll your child

1

Submit Application

Complete the online application form or visit our school to collect and submit a physical application form.

2

Submit Documents

Provide all required documents including birth certificate, medical records, and previous school records (if applicable).

3

Assessment/Interview

For certain levels, students may need to complete a simple assessment or interview with school staff.

4

Complete Registration

Upon acceptance, complete registration by paying required fees and attending orientation.

Admission Requirements

Nursery School Requirements

  • Child must be 3-5 years old
  • Birth certificate (original and copy)
  • Medical record/immunization card
  • Two recent passport-size photos
  • Parent/guardian identification documents
  • Completed application form

Primary School Requirements

  • Child must be 6 years or older for Primary 1
  • Birth certificate (original and copy)
  • Previous school report/transfer certificate (for students joining above P1)
  • Medical record/immunization card
  • Three recent passport-size photos
  • Parent/guardian identification documents
  • Completed application form
  • Simple assessment for placement (for transfer students)

Secondary School Requirements

  • Primary Leaving Certificate or equivalent
  • Birth certificate (original and copy)
  • Previous school report cards and transfer certificate
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Three recent passport-size photos
  • Parent/guardian identification documents
  • Completed application form
  • Entrance examination (for Senior 1)
  • Stream selection form (Sciences, Commercial, or Arts)
